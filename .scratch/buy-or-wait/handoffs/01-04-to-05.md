## 5. Architecture / invariants Ticket 05 must preserve

Do not redesign these. Build Ticket 05 inside them.

- **Deterministic engine owns every output column.** The LLM/extraction layer emits closed-enum `Fact` values only; it never decides.
- **Extraction is an evidence boundary.** `ExtractionPort` is the single external/LLM boundary. The core seam is `run_pipeline(dataset, extraction_facts, config) -> tuple[OutputRow, ...]`.
- **Pure core.** `run_pipeline`, `cash_position`, and recurrence logic must perform no filesystem, network, or clock access.
- **Exact settlement-date FX.** Foreign-currency events convert at the rate row for `(settlement_date, from_currency, home_currency)`. No inverse rates, no nearest-date fallback.
- **90-day safety model.** `amount_safe_to_pay` is headroom minus the worst cumulative drawdown over `request_date` to `request_date + 90` inclusive.
- **Same-day ordering (debits before credits).** Ticket 06 owns this; Ticket 05 should not re-derive it.
- **Output validator is the sole writer to `output.csv`.** It returns violations rather than raising and substitutes conservative rows on failure.
- **Zero runtime dependencies.** Use stdlib only. `Decimal` from strings; no floats in the ledger.
- **Byte-stable output.** `newline=""`, `lineterminator="\n"`, `str(Decimal)`, deterministic sorts.
- **Config-injected parameters.** Every recurrence parameter must live in `Config` with documented defaults; no inline literals. Ticket 14 will settle values.
- **Notification, not exception.** Validation failures are recorded and the fact/batch continues; do not raise for malformed rows.
- **Agent ownership.** Claude Code owns `code/engine/**` and `code/main.py`. OpenCode owns `code/extraction/**`, `code/eval/**`, `tests/**`, `fixtures/**`.

## 6. Ticket 05 starting context

### What to build

Recurring income and expenses are identified from history and projected into the forecast window. Fixed streams project at their last observed amount on their own day of month; variable essential categories project as a conservative monthly total.

### Key interfaces and files

- `code/engine/cash.py` — already classifies every event into `CashEffect` values. Ticket 05 should consume `CashPosition.effects` (explicit open rows) and layer inferred streams on top.
- `code/engine/types.py` —
  - `Config` already has recurrence placeholders: `lookback_days`, `min_occurrences`, `amount_tolerance_pct`, `amount_tolerance_abs`, `description_similarity`, `variable_spend_estimator`, `project_income_beyond_confirmed`.
  - `CashEffect` carries `category` and `flexibility` for spending-change use later.
- `code/engine/pipeline.py` — currently uses the Ticket 01 naive placeholder. Ticket 05 will feed projected streams into the cash position, then Ticket 06 will simulate forward.

### Calibration-sensitive areas (all in `Config`)

- Lookback window.
- Occurrence threshold.
- Amount tolerance (relative + absolute).
- Date tolerance ladder.
- Variable-spend estimator selector.
- Whether recurring income is projected beyond the explicit confirmed salary row.
- Day-of-month placement / month-end clamping.

### Constraints from CONTEXT.md §4

- Stream key: `(category, direction, event_type)` + normalized description similarity.
- Semi-monthly (1st + 15th) must be modelled as **two monthly streams**, never one 15-day stream.
- Day of month clamped to month end; unit-test February and leap years.
- Explicit future scheduled/pending rows suppress projected occurrences for the same stream/date.
- Variable essential spending (groceries/transport/dining) as a conservative monthly total on observed days-of-month.

### What Ticket 05 must NOT do

- Do not re-classify events from `status`/`direction` — `cash.py` already did this.
- Do not project a stream occurrence onto a date an explicit row already covers.
- Do not implement the simulator, plan generation, ranking, or spending changes — those are Tickets 06–08.
- Do not change `ExtractionPort`, fixtures, or validation rules — those are OpenCode.
- Do not hard-code any recurrence parameter.
